## Frozen scientific behavior

- Exact identity and original-source/date/value/unit/context replay precede
  candidate ADME admission. Unknown or post-cutoff candidate measurements are
  quarantined; candidate clinical/physiological narratives are never numerical
  inputs. Exact cutoff: `2006-12-01T23:59:00Z`.
- Measured, sourced, predicted, assumed and calculated quantities remain distinct.
  A predicted alias cannot masquerade as a measured native input. No default
  therapeutic regimen, binding partner, pKa or organ clearance is manufactured.
  Blood, total-systemic and intrinsic clearance are not hepatic-plasma clearance.
- Reviewed inputs/regimens can feed the existing six native PK-Sim handlers.
  Missing inputs refuse PBPK without blocking independent target investigation.
  Actual native paths determine available tissues; no absent organ is invented.
- All highest-precedence conflicts remain visible. Nominal values are withheld;
  a predeclared bounded discrete design retains every level and reports incomplete
  interaction coverage. Cases retain subject, population, native-path and time
  window identity. Native fresh replay is numerical verification, not biology.
- General projected chemical-neighbour assays nominate bounded target hypotheses.
  Candidate/parent/salt identity is excluded before activity/coordinate retrieval.
  Exact target/species/graph/assay-qualified pocket evidence precedes structural
  evaluation with existing RDKit/Meeko/Vina and blocking verification.
  A reference/neighbor's activity is not candidate potency; a docking score is
  not affinity, selectivity or physiological effect.
- Exposure/activity comparisons require exact species, native compartment,
  units, unbound basis and assay context. Total exposure is never relabeled free.
  A comparison is a molecular hypothesis, not a causal physiological arrow.
- Mechanistic execution requires an applicable model and calibrated transfer,
  exact version/hash/equations, native units/scales/components, original observed
  benchmarks and the declared research gates. Missing links remain unresolved.
  Model-source existence or solver agreement is not scientific certification.
- No real progression thresholds are configured in this demonstration.
  INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE is the correct conservative assessment in their absence.
  Existing reviewed rule support remains intact; no threshold is tuned to a
  candidate. Supported partial signals and all refusals remain auditable.
- Classical construction/preparation/docking/native ODE methods do not warrant
  quantum treatment by availability alone. Qiskit/IBM infrastructure is preserved;
  this investigation submits no IBM jobs.
- The answer assembler and saved-session UI display supported computations,
  uncertainty, missing Human inputs, separate neighbor/reference evidence,
  structural refusals and functional refusals. Persisted scientific evidence is
  not changed by visualization enrichment.
