## What is implemented

- Reviewed historical evidence policy, exact UTC cutoff, conservative date
  precision, exact original measurement/unit/date/context replay, explicit
  exclusions and mandatory original/wrapper distinction. Exact text/HTML,
  unambiguous table/CSV/TSV and dual-extractor + rendered-review PDF paths now
  supplement JSON. Unsupported OCR, merged/nested tables and uncertain spans
  remain refused. See `DEMO2_V3_PROSPECTIVE_PROTOCOL_20261002.md`.
- Identity-matched reviewed structured dossier acquisition with endpoint/species
  validation and measured > sourced > assumed precedence. Prediction aliases do
  not bypass the independently calibrated ADME contract. Conflicts are preserved
  with nominal values withheld, not chosen for a desired outcome.
- Legitimate reviewed development dosing or explicitly reviewed exploratory
  controls; no built-in therapeutic dose. Missing parameterization remains visible.
- Reuse of all six existing native PBPK/population/cross-species/verification
  handlers. Two real, independently replayed Human population cases use admitted
  original-primary plasma binding inputs and explicitly separated unrelated
  numerical controls. This is partial measured-input proof, not qualification of
  the other ADME controls or physiological accuracy.
- Bounded Cartesian/level-covering conflict sensitivity preserves all
  highest-precedence values/provenance and withholds a nominal result. Its
  summary requires full native path, preserved virtual-person physiology and
  equal integration windows. Every conditional exposure retains its case,
  subject and population identity when entering functional transfer evaluation.
- Broader ChEMBL chemistry-neighbour target hypotheses: bounded retrieval,
  independently recomputed chiral fingerprints, exact assay/target/species and
  activity-unit checks, projected metadata, self/parent/salt exclusions before
  activity retrieval and preserved-byte replay. Neighbour activity is never
  relabeled as candidate potency.
- Eligible hypotheses now compose an exact accession/species structure panel,
  preferring an active-neighbour co-crystal. When that is absent, a distinct
  assay-qualified pocket reference may qualify the same exact target. This
  general fallback binds the full reference graph to a direct quantitative
  same-target/same-species assay and disclosed experimental structure. It does
  not borrow the reference's activity as candidate potency. Both routes reuse
  existing native docking + blocking verification.
  Nonmatching/mutated/poor-resolution/unsupported structures are refused. An
  empty or refused panel is not reported as a successfully computed target.
- Exact UniProt function/GO and identity-matched Human Protein Atlas expression
  evidence when available. Modern annotations are not claimed pre-cutoff;
  expression is not a drug-effect claim.
- Compatible unbound exposure vs quantitative candidate activity, with exact
  species, tissue/compartment, units and assay context. No total-to-unbound tissue
  shortcut and no docking-to-potency conversion.
- Established hash-pinned SBML interface, equation-preserving libSBML conversion,
  pinned libRoadRunner/CVODE numerical execution, full curves and independent
  numerical replay. Qualified exposure transfer requires an explicit reviewed
  target/parameter/units/context contract. Duplicate exact experiments execute
  once; missing/unqualified optional engines produce independently checked
  refusals, not manufactured curves.
- Research-grade qualification does not require an external reviewer to begin
  engineering. It does require an eight-gate, hash-bound audit package, actual
  original-primary observed benchmarks, unit/domain validation and calibrated
  parameter transfer before a supported research prediction is admitted.
  Solver agreement alone is not biological validation or clinical certification.
- Reviewed computational progression criteria can express ADVANCE, CONCERN,
  REJECT or INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE at their stated nonclinical scope. There are no
  built-in biological decision thresholds or default advancement.
  Critical missing gates, required model refusals or incomplete uncertainty-case
  coverage force INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE even if a partial response crosses a
  criterion; that partial signal remains available in the audit.
- Scientist-facing Virtual Investigation UI reuses the existing molecular and
  native-organism viewers, separates all inference levels and displays actual
  results or explicit missing/refused computations. Saved-session reopening and
  artifact downloads are tested. The explicit-PDB path no longer fails merely
  because automatic structure-selection metadata are absent.
  Exact scientist-supplied deposited chain labels are honored without selecting
  a crystal copy by order. Native receptor failure messages now retain stderr
  diagnoses rather than displaying empty stdout as the reason.
